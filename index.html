Labs · Fariborz Aref

Interactive demonstrations in inequality and method.

Two labs that turn central ideas of sociology into models you can run. The Inequality Lab works with published data on income, wealth, and welfare states. The Methods Lab uses simulation to show how sound calculations lead to unsound conclusions.

They are written for students, colleagues, and anyone who wants to see an argument work rather than take it on trust.
